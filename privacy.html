Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Data Collection & Registration

CockroachTalk does not collect personal identifiers such as names, emails, phone numbers, or passwords. Your chosen species display name and avatar preferences are stored exclusively in your local web browser's localStorage.

2. Audio Processing

Real-time voice communication is powered by encrypted WebRTC connections. Audio streams flow transiently between participants and are never recorded, transcribed, or stored on disk.

3. Cookies & Tracking

We do not use tracking cookies, third-party advertising scripts, or invasive analytics pixels.

4. Microphone Permissions

Microphone access is requested only when you choose to unmute or join the stage, and can be revoked anytime via your browser settings.
