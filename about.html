About CockroachTalk

CockroachTalk is a state-cockroach-themed live voice-rooms platform engineered for speed, privacy, and frictionless community interaction. Every state has a cockroach room—allowing users to join regional audio junctions like Maharashtra Cockroach, Delhi Cockroach, or Karnataka Cockroach with instant display handles and jump right onto the live stage.

Core Principles

  • Zero Login Requirement: No passwords, phone numbers, or email registrations needed.
  • Plain Web Performance: Built entirely with clean standard HTML5, CSS3, and ES6 JavaScript. Under 50KB total script overhead.
  • Privacy First: Audio streams are processed transiently in memory without being archived or monetized.
  • Accessibility & Responsive UX: Full keyboard navigation support, high contrast visual elements, and smooth mobile touch controls.

Roach Pulse Aesthetic

Designed with dark chitin and amber tones, bold poster typography, gold speaking halo indicators, and live pulse badges, CockroachTalk turns every state voice room into an energetic regional audio junction.
